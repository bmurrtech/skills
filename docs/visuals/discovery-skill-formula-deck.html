The formula

The formula and the priority labels.

Business value

BV = ((K + P) / 6) × 100

Delivery readiness

DR = ((F + S) / 6) × 100

Overall priority

Overall = (BV × 0.40) + (DR × 0.60)

Example: K = 3, P = 3, F = 2, S = 3 → BV = 100, DR = 83.3, Overall = 90.0 · P0.
