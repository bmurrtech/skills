# Prioritization

Use when a follow-up, prioritize, or priority conversation is happening and a
list of pains or use cases already exists. Recall that list from the bundle or
attachments. Do not rediscover pain unless they add a new one.

First, confirm **category** with the room for each candidate:

- **External:** customers, partners, the public, or the rest of the organization
- **Internal:** the sponsoring team's own productivity or business process

Then ask the priority choice in more than one wording until they converge or
explicitly decline. Record every frame and highlight the one that settled it.

Wording set (use their language; skip frames that do not fit):

1. If we could only do one first, which gives the organization the most back?
2. Which one improves the most people's work?
3. Which one saves the most time?
4. Which one is low enough risk that a win would build trust for the rest?
5. Which one could another team reuse?

Also capture when said: overlap, later window, "we can already finish this,"
thin first phase, enablement instead of build, absent decider (role, not name).

Do not open requirements questions here. If they volunteer a constraint that
changes the rank, record it.
