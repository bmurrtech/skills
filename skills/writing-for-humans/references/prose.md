# Prose

Read when mode is **Prose**, when a Mixed section explains/reasons, or when
job is **Detect**.

## Edit principles

- Preserve the writer's voice: vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor,
  uncertainty, digressions, polish level. Minimum effective edit.
- Lead with the point when setup adds nothing; keep personal asides that
  create context, tension, or character.
- Keep meaning. Do not invent claims, examples, stats, or opinions.
- Open it up; do not dumb it down. Cut only what blocks reading.
- Concrete specifics beat abstractions. Direct verbs beat weak phrases.
- Portability test: if a sentence could move unchanged to another
  person/product, cut it or make it specific.
- Show; do not tell the reader what to think. Protect specific facts.
- Preserve useful edge. Keep structure unless it hurts the piece.
- Untangle only genuinely hard sentences; leave clear spoken cadence alone.

## Words to cut

Banned when empty: delve, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, empower,
streamline, robust, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, tapestry,
realm, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, intricate, paramount,
transformative, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness, ever-evolving.

Cut often-empty adverbs/phrases (`just`, `literally`, `it's worth noting`,
`in today's world`, `let's dive in`, …) when they delay the point. Keep
when they are real voice, uncertainty, or emphasis.

## Patterns to cut

Binary contrasts; throat-clearing openers; faux-insight setups; dramatic
colon reveals; trailing `-ing` “analysis” (`highlighting`, `underscoring`);
importance puffery; interpretive metadiscourse (`the key point is`); weasel
attribution without sources; fake-strong verbs; synonym cycling; negative
listing; dramatic fragmentation; robotic rhythm; rhetorical setups;
fake-profound kickers; summary-recap endings; formatting slop (emoji
headings, decorative bold, bullet-for-prose); em-dash clusters.

Judge by harm vs help — keep a pattern when it is the writer's real voice
and still earns its place.

## Detect job

Name each matching pattern, quote the line, give a fix in a few words.
Do not rewrite, score, or claim AI authorship. Offer to Edit after.

## Do not over-apply to instructions

Never let prose cuts remove required procedural repetition, warnings,
verification, exact technical terms, or conditional branches.
